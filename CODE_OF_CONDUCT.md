# Code of conduct

Be respectful to the people who use this school system and to the people who build it.

- Do not harass, insult, or publish someone's private school data.
- Do not commit secrets, student records, or signing keys.
- Assume good intent, and describe the change you want.

Reports of a conduct problem go to the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove a comment, a change, or a person's access to the repository.
