# Role guide

## Admin

The school owner turns modules on at `/modules`, plugins at `/plugins`, and themes at `/themes`. The owner can trust or revoke a publisher key. Other roles do not see package upload. Students, staff, classes, fees, attendance, and the other school screens are modules that ship with the system. Roles list each module's permissions.

## Teacher

A teacher sees the classes assigned for the active year. Attendance for those classes is under Attendance. Marks and lesson plans follow the same assignment. A teacher does not see the package upload section.

## Finance

Finance holds fee items, invoices, payments, and receipts. Recording a payment does not call a mobile-money provider. Fee reminders are sent from Fee reminders when that plugin is on, and the receipt says the message was not sent until a gateway exists.

## Student

A student with an account sees their own portal and linked records. They do not manage other students.

## Parent

A parent sees the students linked to their guardian account. Absence messages in the inbox are for those students. SMS and email copies are recorded only when those plugins are on, and they are not delivered to a phone or inbox outside the app.
